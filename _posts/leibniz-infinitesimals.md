From Google:

1. The Czech-American School: Hrbacek and the "Theory of Levels" (FRST / SPOTS)This group is led by the mathematician Karel Hrbacek (often writing alongside Olivier Lessmann or Mikhail Katz). https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.00621

How they overcame your objection: They realized that standard Robinson non-standard analysis forces you into a single, flat world where you have "standard numbers" and "nonstandard numbers." To fix this, Hrbacek introduced FRST (Fully Relative Set Theory) and SPOTS 

2. The Italian School: Giordano and "Generalized Smooth Functions" (Fermat Reals)This group is led by Paolo Giordano (often collaborating with Michael Grosser or Mikhail Katz). They took an algebraic approach, specifically aiming to fix the flaws of Smooth Infinitesimal Analysis (SIA)

How they overcame your objection: They created a framework known as The Fermat Reals or Generalized Smooth Functions. They wanted the extreme ease of calculating derivatives that you get in SIA, but they refused to accept that infinitesimals must be nilpotent

By the Hrbacek School: “Nonstandard set theories with multiple levels of standardness” (Hrbacek, 2021/2024), which explicitly lays out the hierarchical layers of smallness.By the Giordano School: “Fermat reals: Nilpotent infinitesimals without intuitionistic logic” or “Potential and actual infinitesimals in models of continuum” (Giordano & Katz, 2011/2020), which explicitly discusses bypassing the nilpotent restrictions of category-theoretic continuum models.
